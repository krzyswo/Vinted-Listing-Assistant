Common questions

What the project does — and what it deliberately avoids

The assistant focuses on preparation quality. Publishing and final verification remain manual.

Does it publish listings automatically?+

No. It does not log in to Vinted, click through the website, send messages, refresh listings, or publish on your behalf. It prepares a draft and export package for manual use.

Can it run entirely with local AI?+

Yes. Configure an OpenAI-compatible server such as LM Studio, choose a vision model for photo analysis and a text model for writing, and keep the workflow on your own machine or local network.

Why use two local models?+

Photo understanding and polished writing are different tasks. InternVL can focus on visible product details, while Qwen can focus on clear language, correction, and structured listing output.

Can Azure OpenAI remain configured at the same time?+

Yes. Local model settings and Azure settings are stored separately, so you can switch providers without losing the other configuration.

Is AI output always accurate?+

No. Photo analysis cannot confirm authenticity, hidden defects, or facts that are not visible. Every title, description, category, brand, material, condition, and price must be reviewed before publication.

Is this an official Vinted product?+

No. This is an independent open-source project and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Vinted.
